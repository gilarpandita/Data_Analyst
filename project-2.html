Brewing Engagement: Finding What Drives Instagram Engagement for a Yogyakarta Chocolate Café

The CSV contains 16 Instagram posts (Oct 5 to Nov 9, 2026) with a total reach of 35,433 and 778 engagements. Engagement rate (ER) in this file is engagements divided by reach. Median ER per post is 3.7%, and the pooled ER is 2.2%. Of the 16 posts, 14 are organic, 1 is boosted, and 1 is a collaboration. These two paid or partnered posts matter because they distort the reach picture.

Key Findings

  • Reach is highly concentrated. The top three posts account for 79% of total reach: the boosted post (18,219), the collaboration (5,949), and one organic Halloween-themed post (3,804). Median reach for organic posts is only about 560.
  • High reach does not mean high engagement. The boosted post had the largest reach but the lowest ER (0.76%). Reach and ER are negatively correlated in this dataset (-0.52), because low-reach posts automatically get inflated ER. ER alone is not enough, so shares, saves, and profile visits should be read alongside it.
  • Format: Reels have a median ER of 5.2% versus 3.4% for images, but there are only 2 Reels out of 16 posts. This is a promising direction, not a conclusion.
  • Content pillar: Menu Spotlight is the most frequent pillar (9 posts) with a solid median ER of 4.0%, but its reach is low (median 424) and it earns almost no shares or saves. The Halloween-themed Community Engagement posts are the strongest on shares (median 40), saves, and profile visits, though only 2 of them are organic.
  • Posting time: The 2-4 PM slot has the highest ER (median 5.0%) but the lowest reach (329). The 11 AM-1 PM slot has higher reach and produced 6 of the 7 new followers. There are too few posts per weekday to compare days.
  • Caption style: Longer captions tend to have lower ER (correlation -0.45). Captions of 36-70 words have a median ER of 3.1%, versus 4.0% for 16-35 words. Captions with a question mark scored lower (2.9% vs 4.0%). This may reflect tone or topic rather than cause and effect.

Initial recommendations

  • Publish more Reels and track them separately, since there are only 2 samples so far.
  • Keep Menu Spotlight, but borrow the Community Engagement approach that drives shares and profile visits, such as seasonal or story-driven themes.
  • Test shorter captions (under 35 words) against longer ones on the same topic.
  • Report ER together with shares, saves, and profile visits, so high reach from boosting doesn’t mask organic performance.

Caveats

  • The sample is very small (16 posts over 5 weeks), so everything here is indicative. In your portfolio, describe it as a pilot analysis.
  • Posts from early November may not have accumulated their full reach yet, so avoid reading a decline into the November numbers.
  • The Campaign, Views, and UTM columns are empty, so the impact of campaigns like Rabo Italiano or Sunday Chill can’t be measured from this file.
